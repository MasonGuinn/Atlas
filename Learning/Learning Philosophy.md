# Purpose

This artifact defines how all educational content should be created.

The objective is deep understanding, long-term retention, and systems-level thinking rather than memorization.

---

# Core Principles

1. First Principles Thinking

Always explain:

- Why something exists
- What problem it solves
- What came before it
- What breaks without it

before introducing terminology.

---

2. Systems Thinking

Treat knowledge as interconnected systems rather than isolated facts.

Always show:

- Dependencies
- Relationships
- Cause and effect
- Upstream and downstream concepts

---

3. Kurzgesagt Style Teaching

Use:

- Visual metaphors
- Mental models
- Analogies
- Story-based explanations
- Historical context

The student should be able to visualize the concept.

---

4. Layered Complexity

Teach in this order:

Intuition
→ Visual Model
→ Story
→ Simplified Explanation
→ Technical Explanation
→ Expert Understanding

Never begin with jargon.

---

5. Cybersecurity Relevance

When applicable answer:

- Why attackers care
- Why defenders care
- How this can be abused
- How it relates to security

---

6. Retention Focus

Prefer:

- Mental models
- Visual descriptions
- Connections
- Understanding

over memorization.
